Overview

Tippy Card Monte is a small pixel card game: a shuffled row, a slightly smug mascot, and a bet that gets harder every round. It is built to be played in a browser tab for five minutes, not installed.

How it plays

  • Watch Tippy shuffle, then pick the card they're under
  • Correct picks bank Tippy Coins, wrong picks take them away
  • The deck grows from 3 to 15 cards across 15 rounds
  • Stay in the green — going into debt ends the run
  • Everything runs locally: no accounts, no backend, no network calls
