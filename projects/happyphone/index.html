What HappyPhone is

HappyPhone is a small startup that puts real helpers on the phone for American businesses. The pitch is deliberately simple: a clear offer, a simple six-column sheet, real humans, and honest math.

I'm on the team, and my corner of it is the internal tooling the callers actually touch.

My role: the internal softphone

Every caller needs their own login, their own extension, and their own call list. I'm building the web app that gives them exactly that — plus the gateway that gets the audio out to the phone network.

  • Caller sign-in with three fields, always in the same order: username, extension, password
  • Per-caller call lists, filled by drag-and-drop CSV or a pasted Google Sheet link
  • Secure wss gateway on Kamailio + RTPEngine, routing outbound calls through the team's VoIP.ms sub-account
  • JWT-based auth — raw SIP passwords never live in the browser
  • Every call recorded and logged into the team's sheet

Where it lives

The real home will be caller.happyphone.lol, with the login at #/login-webui and the dashboard at #/dashboard. Until then, the working prototype is the HappyFone WebUI below.
