# Builds

19 entries — projects, releases and the notes written after them.

  1. Nov 3, 2026

    AdBlock Island gets a real project page and live patch notes ↗

    v0.6.7 beta now has its own page with screenshots, a download and the full changelog rendered as patch notes — version cards, BETA pills and collapsible releases. “Still pending” moved into its own section with a Pending button, and the game’s title-screen PATCH NOTES button now opens the styled page instead of raw GitHub Markdown.

  2. Nov 2, 2026

    job-scrapper moves off Make.com onto Discord + Gemini ↗

    The scraper now summarizes each OnlineJobs.ph listing with Gemini and posts one Discord embed per job, with URL dedupe and a 4-hour cadence. No more Make.com hop — the whole pipeline runs in GitHub Actions.

  3. Oct 28, 2026

    New post: the invoice chaser, three bugs, one rule ↗

    Write-up of what actually broke while building the AR agent: Gemini returning an empty response to a structured parser because the token budget went to reasoning, a signature the model kept rewriting (now enforced in code), a daily schedule that chased the same invoice every morning (now a 7-day cooldown), and a sentence counter that counted dollar signs.

  4. Oct 28, 2026

    AR Invoice Reminder Agent ships ↗

    A daily n8n workflow that reads an invoice tracker, computes days past due, and drafts a tone-matched payment reminder with Gemini — friendly under 14 days, firm to 30, solution-oriented after. Every email lands in Gmail as a draft, the signature is rebuilt in code, a 7-day cooldown stops duplicate chasing, and the follow-up date is written back to the sheet. Verified end-to-end on a live tracker.

  5. Oct 28, 2026

    Homepage refocused on automation ↗

    The site now leads with the work: the voice receptionist stays the clinic flagship, packages start with an Automation Sprint for any repetitive process, and a three-step process section replaces the dotfiles setup. Non-automation side projects and posts moved off the homepage.

  6. Oct 25, 2026

    Voice receptionist moves to Retell — live on synthetic calls ↗

    The shared voice layer is now Retell: call_analyzed webhooks, an immediate 200 ack, and call_id dedupe before booking. The n8n branch ran end-to-end on three synthetic calls with real Google Sheets logging and Gmail alerts; docs, exports, simulator payloads and the workflow screenshot were rebuilt around the new contract.

  7. Oct 25, 2026

    Cal.com fit-call booking goes live ↗

    Every “book a fit call” CTA now opens a real 60-minute Cal.com page instead of a placeholder — wired through the homepage, packages section and voice-receptionist README.

  8. Oct 24, 2026

    n8n voice exports repaired — Sheets + Gmail live ↗

    Both exported workflows now import cleanly: valid JSON, restored wiring and expressions, real Google Sheets call logging and Gmail staff alerts. Calendar creation is documented as an API-credential blocker instead of failing silently.

  9. Oct 24, 2026

    Dead Lead Follow-up becomes Dead Lead Retrieval ↗

    Renamed the project everywhere — repo, profile, site and n8n export — to match what it actually does: resurrect dead leads with approved Gmail drafts and ClickUp tracking.

  10. Oct 23, 2026

    Work with me packages + clinic demo ↗

    Added three engagement tiers (Automation Sprint, Voice Receptionist, HIPAA Build) and an interactive clinic demo. The AI Voice Receptionist is now featured across the homepage with the HIPAA-compliant build badge.

  11. Sep 10, 2026

    rss-news goes live ↗

    Weekly AI & tech digest automation: Python fetches Hacker News, Phoronix, The Verge and friends, filters by keyword, and ships the digest to Make.com for Gemini drafting, Imagen graphics and Facebook Pages publishing.

  12. Aug 29, 2026

  14. Aug 25, 2026

  15. Aug 25, 2026
