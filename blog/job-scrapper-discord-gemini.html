job-scrapper now posts to Discord with Gemini summaries

Nov 2, 2026 / 6 min read / 177 words / Christian John
PythonScrapingLLM
On this page

    The scraper used to push raw job payloads to a Make.com webhook and let the scenario do the rest. That worked, but it meant a second platform in the loop and a secret I had to keep in sync. Now the whole thing runs inside GitHub Actions: scrape OnlineJobs.ph, summarize each listing with Gemini, post one Discord embed per job.

    What changed

    • Discord instead of Make.com: each job becomes a rich embed (title, category, rate, type, posted date, how-to-apply, link).
    • Gemini summaries: a short 2–4 sentence blurb per listing, with a fallback to raw fields if the API fails.
    • Dedupe: seen_jobs.json remembers sent URLs so repeat runs don't spam the channel.
    • Cadence: every 4 hours, with randomized delays to stay polite.

    Why 4 hours and not 10 minutes

    OnlineJobs.ph has anti-bot interstitials. Hitting it every 10 minutes is a great way to get blocked. Four hours keeps listings fresh without tripping rate limits, and dedupe keeps the channel clean.

    Secrets needed

    • DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL
    • GEMINI_API_KEY
    • Optional: GEMINI_MODEL (default gemini-1.5-flash), CATEGORIES, MAX_PER_CATEGORY
